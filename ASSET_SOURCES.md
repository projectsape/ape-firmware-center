## T147 board photography

- Board: Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.47.
- Product page and purchase link: https://www.waveshare.com/esp32-s3-touch-lcd-1.47.htm
- Original image supplied by Enmanuel and preserved as `public/assets/boards/originals/t147-user-provided.jpg`.
- Web derivative: `public/assets/boards/t147.webp` — background made transparent via
  border flood-fill (near-black background connected to the image borders is
  removed; the board bezel and LCD content stay opaque), feathered and cropped.
- The same hardware photograph identifies both APE firmware variants; the UI distinguishes external-SX1262 LoRa and NoLoRa behavior in text.
- Waveshare product imagery and trademarks remain with Waveshare.
